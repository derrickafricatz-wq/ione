# I|ONE Sokoni User Guide / Mwongozo wa Matumizi wa I|ONE Sokoni

This guide covers the current Sokoni food-market and Boda Yangu workflows in I|ONE. The Sokoni interface is Swahili-first; the guide is provided in English and Kiswahili. Authority approval is required where stated.

Mwongozo huu unaeleza matumizi ya Sokoni na Boda Yangu ndani ya I|ONE. Mfumo wa Sokoni umeandaliwa kwa Kiswahili; mwongozo huu umetolewa kwa Kiingereza na Kiswahili. Ruhusa ya Authority Press inahitajika pale ilipoelezwa.

## ENGLISH

### 1. What is Sokoni?
Sokoni brings nearby food and market products, local food sellers, buyers, and Boda Yangu transport together in one place. The main areas are **Mama Sokoni**, **Mama wa Pishi**, and **Boda Yangu**.

### 2. For Mama Sokoni (market vendors)
1. Open Sokoni and submit your name and Tanzania phone number to apply.
2. Wait for Authority Press to review and approve your vendor access. Approval is not automatic.
3. Once approved and active, add your vendor profile information and profile photo.
4. Add products with their names, prices, and product photos where available.
5. Update your market presence using **Nipo Sokoni / Sipo Sokoni** so customers can tell whether you are available.
6. Keep product prices and availability accurate. Manage incoming orders from the Sokoni panel.
7. Vendor access is TZS 1,000 for 30 days and must be activated by Authority Press. Check your displayed status and expiry date.

### 3. For buyers
1. Open Sokoni and browse available vendors and products.
2. You can choose more than one product from a vendor and add the items to one combined order.
3. Check quantities and the order details before submitting.
4. If the vendor accepts the order, check the order status and follow the instructions shown in Sokoni.
5. If you choose delivery, provide a clear delivery location/address. GPS may be optional; provide it only if the app offers it and you wish to use it.
6. Keep your phone reachable so you can receive updates or contact the vendor/rider when needed.

### 4. Delivery with Boda Yangu
1. When a vendor accepts an order that needs delivery, the system can create a delivery request for eligible, verified riders who are available.
2. Riders can review the delivery details and accept an available request. The buyer's purchase total is not shown to the rider.
3. The buyer and vendor should confirm the delivery fee through the options shown in the app before the delivery proceeds.
4. Buyer and vendor can contact each other or the rider to coordinate pickup and drop-off.
5. The rider updates the delivery status in the app and marks the delivery complete when the goods have been handed over.
6. Rider availability is managed around accepted work; riders should finish the trip before returning to available status.

### 5. For Boda Yangu riders
1. Submit a rider application and wait for Authority Press approval.
2. Provide the required profile details, motorcycle plate number, group/chairman details, and one accepted identity document: driving licence, national ID, or boda group ID, as requested by the form.
3. Rider registration costs TZS 5,000 for three months. Authority Press controls approval and access dates.
4. Keep your profile and phone number current. Set your availability accurately and accept only work you can complete.
5. For deliveries, follow the pickup and delivery instructions, update the trip status, and contact Mama Sokoni or the buyer when necessary.
6. For passenger trips, review the request on the Boda Yangu board, accept only when ready, contact the passenger as needed, start the trip, and mark it complete at the end.

### 6. Notifications and troubleshooting
- Check the Sokoni panel and order status if a notification does not appear.
- Keep your phone number correct and your phone able to receive messages.
- If an order or delivery status seems stuck, refresh the panel and contact the other party before creating a duplicate order.
- If a vendor or rider application is pending or access has expired, contact Authority Press.
- Never share your password, verification codes, or private identity-document links.

### 7. Authority Press
Authority Press reviews vendor and rider applications, approves or rejects access, and manages access periods. An application does not mean that access has been granted.

## KISWAHILI

### 1. Sokoni ni nini?
Sokoni inaunganisha bidhaa za sokoni, wauzaji wa vyakula, wanunuzi na usafiri wa Boda Yangu katika sehemu moja. Sehemu kuu ni **Mama Sokoni**, **Mama wa Pishi**, na **Boda Yangu**.

### 2. Kwa Mama Sokoni (wauzaji wa sokoni)
1. Fungua Sokoni na tuma maombi kwa kuweka jina lako na namba ya simu ya Tanzania.
2. Subiri Authority Press ikague na kuidhinisha maombi yako. Ruhusa haitolewi moja kwa moja.
3. Baada ya kuidhinishwa na akaunti kuwa hai, jaza taarifa za wasifu wa biashara na weka picha ya wasifu.
4. Ongeza bidhaa kwa kuweka majina, bei na picha za bidhaa inapowezekana.
5. Tumia **Nipo Sokoni / Sipo Sokoni** kuonyesha kama upo tayari kuhudumia wateja.
6. Hakikisha bei na upatikanaji wa bidhaa ni sahihi. Simamia oda zinazoingia kupitia paneli ya Sokoni.
7. Ada ya matumizi ya Mama Sokoni ni TZS 1,000 kwa siku 30 na huwashwa na Authority Press. Angalia hali ya akaunti na tarehe ya mwisho wa ruhusa.

### 3. Kwa wanunuzi
1. Fungua Sokoni na angalia wauzaji pamoja na bidhaa zinazopatikana.
2. Unaweza kuchagua bidhaa zaidi ya moja kutoka kwa muuzaji mmoja na kuziweka katika oda moja.
3. Kagua idadi ya bidhaa na taarifa za oda kabla ya kuituma.
4. Muuzaji akikubali oda, angalia hali ya oda na fuata maelekezo yanayoonekana ndani ya Sokoni.
5. Ukichagua kufikishiwa bidhaa, weka eneo au anwani iliyoeleweka. GPS inaweza kuwa ya hiari; itumie tu kama mfumo unatoa chaguo hilo na unataka kuitumia.
6. Hakikisha simu yako inapatikana ili upokee taarifa au uwasiliane na muuzaji/dereva inapohitajika.

### 4. Usafirishaji kupitia Boda Yangu
1. Muuzaji akikubali oda inayohitaji usafirishaji, mfumo unaweza kutengeneza ombi la usafirishaji na kuwajulisha madereva waliothibitishwa, wenye ruhusa na walio tayari.
2. Dereva anaweza kuona maelezo ya usafirishaji na kukubali ombi linalopatikana. Jumla ya pesa alizotumia mnunuzi kununua bidhaa haionyeshwi kwa dereva.
3. Mnunuzi na muuzaji wanapaswa kuthibitisha ada ya usafirishaji kupitia chaguo zinazoonekana kwenye mfumo kabla ya usafirishaji kuendelea.
4. Mnunuzi na muuzaji wanaweza kuwasiliana wao kwa wao au na dereva kupanga kuchukua na kupeleka bidhaa.
5. Dereva husasisha hali ya usafirishaji kwenye mfumo na kuweka kuwa umekamilika baada ya bidhaa kukabidhiwa.
6. Upatikanaji wa dereva husimamiwa kulingana na kazi alizokubali; amalize safari kabla ya kurudi kwenye hali ya kupatikana.

### 5. Kwa madereva wa Boda Yangu
1. Tuma maombi ya udereva na subiri idhini ya Authority Press.
2. Weka taarifa zinazohitajika za wasifu, namba ya usajili wa pikipiki, taarifa za kikundi/mwenyekiti, na kitambulisho kimoja kinachokubalika: leseni ya udereva, kitambulisho cha taifa au kitambulisho cha kikundi cha bodaboda, kulingana na fomu.
3. Ada ya usajili ni TZS 5,000 kwa miezi mitatu. Authority Press ndiyo hudhibiti idhini na tarehe za ruhusa.
4. Hakikisha taarifa za wasifu na namba ya simu ni sahihi. Onyesha hali yako ya upatikanaji kwa usahihi na kubali kazi unazoweza kukamilisha.
5. Kwa usafirishaji wa bidhaa, fuata maelekezo ya kuchukua na kupeleka bidhaa, sasisha hali ya safari na wasiliana na Mama Sokoni au mnunuzi inapohitajika.
6. Kwa safari za abiria, kagua ombi kwenye ubao wa Boda Yangu, kubali ukiwa tayari, wasiliana na abiria inapohitajika, anza safari na uweke kuwa imekamilika mwisho wa safari.

### 6. Taarifa na utatuzi wa matatizo
- Ikiwa taarifa haijaonekana, angalia paneli ya Sokoni na hali ya oda.
- Hakikisha namba yako ya simu ni sahihi na simu inaweza kupokea ujumbe.
- Ikiwa hali ya oda au usafirishaji haibadiliki, onyesha upya paneli na wasiliana na mhusika mwingine kabla ya kuunda oda nyingine.
- Ikiwa ombi la muuzaji au dereva bado linasubiri, au muda wa ruhusa umeisha, wasiliana na Authority Press.
- Usimpe mtu nenosiri, misimbo ya uthibitisho au viungo vya faragha vya nyaraka za utambulisho.

### 7. Authority Press
Authority Press hukagua maombi ya wauzaji na madereva, huidhinisha au kukataa ruhusa na kusimamia muda wa ruhusa. Kutuma ombi hakumaanishi kuwa ruhusa imetolewa.
