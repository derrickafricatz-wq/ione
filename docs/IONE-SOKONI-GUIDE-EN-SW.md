# I|ONE Sokoni User Guide / Mwongozo wa Matumizi wa I|ONE Sokoni

This guide covers the current Sokoni food-market and Boda Yangu workflows in I|ONE. The Sokoni interface is Swahili-first; the guide is provided in English and Kiswahili. Authority approval is required where stated.

Mwongozo huu unaeleza matumizi ya Sokoni na Boda Yangu ndani ya I|ONE. Mfumo wa Sokoni umeandaliwa kwa Kiswahili; mwongozo huu umetolewa kwa Kiingereza na Kiswahili. Ruhusa ya Authority Press inahitajika pale ilipoelezwa.

## ENGLISH

### 1. What is Sokoni?
Sokoni brings nearby food and market products, local food sellers, buyers, and Boda Yangu transport together in one place. The main areas are **Mama Sokoni**, **Mama wa Pishi**, and **Boda Yangu**.

### 2. I1 Opportunities (jobs and other opportunities)
I1 Opportunities is a noticeboard for **Jobs, Scholarships, Training, Tenders, and Business** opportunities. Listings shown to the public are published by I|ONE and should include the source's details and deadline where available.

**For people looking for opportunities**
1. Open **I1 Opportunities** from the I|ONE homepage.
2. Browse the listings or search by title, organization, or location.
3. Use the category filters to focus on Jobs, Scholarships, Training, Tenders, or Business.
4. Open a listing, read the details and deadline carefully, then use its official source link or contact details to apply or follow up. I|ONE displays opportunities; the original organization controls its application process.
5. Check deadlines and requirements on the original source before sharing personal information or paying any fee.

**For organizations or people who want I|ONE to publish an opportunity**
- You can request publication of genuine jobs, scholarships, training, tenders, business opportunities, and similar notices.
- The displayed publication offer is **US$4 for unlimited opportunity listings**.
- Contact I|ONE using **Call I|ONE**, **Chat I|ONE · WhatsApp**, or **Chat I|ONE Support** shown inside the Opportunities feature. Confirm the details and payment arrangements directly with I|ONE before proceeding.
- Submitting a request does not mean the listing is already published. Public listings are managed through Authority Press.

### 3. I1 Smart Ads (for businesses and advertisers)
I1 Smart Ads lets advertisers submit campaigns to promote a business, product, or service in selected parts of I|ONE. Available placements include Smart Ads, Explore, Book Reader, Documentary, Expert, and **Sokoni marketplace**.

1. Open **I1 Smart Ads** and choose **Create Campaign**. Fill in the advertiser/business name, contact details, ad title and description.
2. Upload an image or video: JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, or WEBM. Maximum file size is 50 MB; videos must be 2 minutes or shorter.
3. Choose a campaign goal, target city/area, and where the ad should appear. Select **Sokoni marketplace** if you want to reach people browsing Sokoni. Add a destination link if you have one.
4. Choose a campaign period: **7 days — TZS 2,000; 30 days — TZS 5,000; or 90 days — TZS 10,000**.
5. You can add creatives within the package limit: weekly up to 2, monthly up to 5, or quarterly up to 10. Set daily start/end times in Tanzania time if you want the ad shown only during certain hours.
6. Submit the campaign and follow the payment instructions shown by I|ONE. The campaign remains pending until payment is verified and Authority Press approves/activates it.
7. Check **My Campaigns** to view its status and available performance counts, such as impressions, clicks, contact actions, and reported conversions. A submitted campaign is not guaranteed to appear until approved, paid/verified, and within its scheduled dates and placement.
8. If you connect an I1 Marketing brand, only an active billboard owned by your account can be linked. Keep your contact information and destination link correct.

### 4. Smart Ads in Sokoni (for businesses and advertisers)
Sokoni now supports **Smart Ads** campaigns. An advertiser can request an ad to appear in the Sokoni marketplace, helping promote a business, product, or service to people browsing Sokoni.

1. Open the I|ONE Smart Ads feature and create a campaign.
2. Add campaign images or videos. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, and WEBM. Each file can be up to 50 MB; video creatives can be up to 2 minutes.
3. Choose how many creatives your campaign package allows: weekly up to 2, monthly up to 5, or quarterly up to 10.
4. Set the campaign goal: website visits, customer contacts, or brand awareness. Add a target city/area and an optional destination link.
5. Under ad placement, select **Sokoni marketplace** to request placement in Sokoni, or select all eligible placements if that is what you want.
6. You may set a daily start and end time using Tanzania local time. Leave both blank for all-day scheduling.
7. Submit the campaign and wait for approval. Ads display only when approved, active within their campaign dates, and eligible for the selected placement and schedule. Submission does not guarantee immediate display.
8. If you link an I1 Marketing brand, only an active billboard owned by your account can be linked. If no brand is linked, the View Brand option is hidden; Learn More can use the destination link or available contact route.

### 5. For Mama Sokoni (market vendors)
1. Open Sokoni and submit your name and Tanzania phone number to apply.
2. Wait for Authority Press to review and approve your vendor access. Approval is not automatic.
3. Once approved and active, add your vendor profile information and profile photo.
4. Add products with their names, prices, and product photos where available.
5. Update your market presence using **Nipo Sokoni / Sipo Sokoni** so customers can tell whether you are available.
6. Keep product prices and availability accurate. Manage incoming orders from the Sokoni panel.
7. Vendor access is TZS 1,000 for 30 days and must be activated by Authority Press. Check your displayed status and expiry date.

### 6. For buyers
1. Open Sokoni and browse available vendors and products.
2. You can choose more than one product from a vendor and add the items to one combined order.
3. Check quantities and the order details before submitting.
4. If the vendor accepts the order, check the order status and follow the instructions shown in Sokoni.
5. If you choose delivery, provide a clear delivery location/address. GPS may be optional; provide it only if the app offers it and you wish to use it.
6. Keep your phone reachable so you can receive updates or contact the vendor/rider when needed.

### 7. Delivery with Boda Yangu
1. When a vendor accepts an order that needs delivery, the system can create a delivery request for eligible, verified riders who are available.
2. Riders can review the delivery details and accept an available request. The buyer's purchase total is not shown to the rider.
3. The buyer and vendor should confirm the delivery fee through the options shown in the app before the delivery proceeds.
4. Buyer and vendor can contact each other or the rider to coordinate pickup and drop-off.
5. The rider updates the delivery status in the app and marks the delivery complete when the goods have been handed over.
6. Rider availability is managed around accepted work; riders should finish the trip before returning to available status.

### 8. For Boda Yangu riders
1. Submit a rider application and wait for Authority Press approval.
2. Provide the required profile details, motorcycle plate number, group/chairman details, and one accepted identity document: driving licence, national ID, or boda group ID, as requested by the form.
3. Rider registration costs TZS 5,000 for three months. Authority Press controls approval and access dates.
4. Keep your profile and phone number current. Set your availability accurately and accept only work you can complete.
5. For deliveries, follow the pickup and delivery instructions, update the trip status, and contact Mama Sokoni or the buyer when necessary.
6. For passenger trips, review the request on the Boda Yangu board, accept only when ready, contact the passenger as needed, start the trip, and mark it complete at the end.

### 9. Notifications and troubleshooting
- Check the Sokoni panel and order status if a notification does not appear.
- Keep your phone number correct and your phone able to receive messages.
- If an order or delivery status seems stuck, refresh the panel and contact the other party before creating a duplicate order.
- If a vendor or rider application is pending or access has expired, contact Authority Press.
- Never share your password, verification codes, or private identity-document links.

### 10. Authority Press
Authority Press reviews vendor and rider applications, approves or rejects access, and manages access periods. An application does not mean that access has been granted.

## KISWAHILI

### 1. Sokoni ni nini?
Sokoni inaunganisha bidhaa za sokoni, wauzaji wa vyakula, wanunuzi na usafiri wa Boda Yangu katika sehemu moja. Sehemu kuu ni **Mama Sokoni**, **Mama wa Pishi**, na **Boda Yangu**.

### 2. I1 Opportunities (kazi na fursa nyingine)
I1 Opportunities ni ubao wa matangazo ya **Ajira, Masomo/Scholarships, Mafunzo, Zabuni, na Biashara**. Fursa zinazoonekana kwa umma huchapishwa na I|ONE na huonyesha chanzo na tarehe ya mwisho pale taarifa hizo zinapopatikana.

**Kwa wanaotafuta fursa**
1. Fungua **I1 Opportunities** kutoka ukurasa wa mwanzo wa I|ONE.
2. Angalia matangazo au tafuta kwa jina la fursa, taasisi au eneo.
3. Tumia vichujio kuchagua Ajira, Scholarships, Mafunzo, Zabuni au Biashara.
4. Fungua tangazo, soma maelezo na tarehe ya mwisho, kisha tumia kiungo rasmi au mawasiliano yaliyowekwa kuomba au kuuliza zaidi. I|ONE huonyesha fursa; taasisi husika ndiyo husimamia mchakato wa maombi.
5. Hakiki masharti na tarehe kwenye chanzo rasmi kabla ya kutoa taarifa binafsi au kulipa ada yoyote.

**Kwa taasisi au mtu anayetaka I|ONE ichapishe fursa yake**
- Unaweza kuomba kuchapishiwa fursa halisi kama ajira, scholarships, mafunzo, zabuni, fursa za biashara na matangazo yanayofanana.
- Bei inayoonyeshwa ya huduma ya uchapishaji ni **US$4 kwa matangazo ya fursa yasiyo na kikomo**.
- Wasiliana na I|ONE kupitia **Call I|ONE**, **Chat I|ONE · WhatsApp**, au **Chat I|ONE Support** zinazoonyeshwa ndani ya Opportunities. Thibitisha maelezo na utaratibu wa malipo moja kwa moja na I|ONE kabla ya kuendelea.
- Kutuma ombi hakumaanishi tangazo tayari limechapishwa. Matangazo ya umma husimamiwa kupitia Authority Press.

### 3. I1 Smart Ads (kwa biashara na watangazaji)
I1 Smart Ads inaruhusu watangazaji kuwasilisha kampeni za kutangaza biashara, bidhaa au huduma katika sehemu zilizochaguliwa za I|ONE. Sehemu zinazopatikana ni Smart Ads, Explore, Book Reader, Documentary, Expert na **Sokoni marketplace**.

1. Fungua **I1 Smart Ads** na uchague **Create Campaign**. Jaza jina la mtangazaji/biashara, mawasiliano, kichwa na maelezo ya tangazo.
2. Pakia picha au video: JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4 au WEBM. Faili lisizidi MB 50; video isiwe zaidi ya dakika 2.
3. Chagua lengo la kampeni, mji/eneo unalolenga na sehemu tangazo litakapoonekana. Chagua **Sokoni marketplace** kama unataka kuwafikia wanaotembelea Sokoni. Ongeza kiungo cha kwenda nacho ikiwa unacho.
4. Chagua muda wa kampeni: **siku 7 — TZS 2,000; siku 30 — TZS 5,000; au siku 90 — TZS 10,000**.
5. Unaweza kuongeza picha/video kulingana na kikomo cha kifurushi: cha wiki hadi 2, cha mwezi hadi 5, au cha robo mwaka hadi 10. Weka muda wa kila siku kwa saa za Tanzania ikiwa hutaki tangazo lionekane siku nzima.
6. Tuma kampeni na fuata maelekezo ya malipo yanayoonyeshwa na I|ONE. Kampeni hubaki kusubiri hadi malipo yathibitishwe na Authority Press iidhinishe/iwashie kampeni.
7. Fungua **My Campaigns** kuona hali ya kampeni na hesabu zinazopatikana kama mara tangazo lilivyoonekana, mibofyo, hatua za mawasiliano na matokeo yaliyoripotiwa. Kampeni haitaanza kuonekana hadi iwe imeidhinishwa, malipo yathibitishwe, na tarehe/muda pamoja na sehemu iliyochaguliwa viwe sahihi.
8. Ukiunganisha chapa ya I1 Marketing, unaweza kuunganisha ubao wa matangazo ulio hai na unaomilikiwa na akaunti yako pekee. Hakikisha mawasiliano na kiungo cha tangazo ni sahihi.

### 4. Smart Ads ndani ya Sokoni (kwa biashara na watangazaji)
Sokoni sasa inaruhusu kampeni za **Smart Ads**. Mtangazaji anaweza kuomba tangazo lake lionekane kwenye soko la Sokoni ili kutangaza biashara, bidhaa au huduma kwa watu wanaotumia Sokoni.

1. Fungua sehemu ya Smart Ads ndani ya I|ONE na tengeneza kampeni.
2. Ongeza picha au video za tangazo. Miundo inayokubalika ni JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4 na WEBM. Kila faili linaweza kuwa hadi MB 50; video inaweza kuwa hadi dakika 2.
3. Idadi ya picha/video inategemea kifurushi: cha wiki hadi 2, cha mwezi hadi 5, au cha robo mwaka hadi 10.
4. Chagua lengo la kampeni: kutembelewa kwa tovuti, mawasiliano kutoka kwa wateja, au kuongeza utambuzi wa chapa. Weka mji/eneo unalolenga na kiungo cha kwenda nacho ikiwa unacho.
5. Kwenye sehemu ya mahali tangazo litaonekana, chagua **Sokoni marketplace** ili kuomba tangazo lionekane Sokoni, au chagua sehemu zote zinazokubalika ikiwa hilo ndilo lengo lako.
6. Unaweza kuweka muda wa kuanza na kumaliza kuonyesha tangazo kila siku kwa saa za Tanzania. Acha sehemu zote mbili wazi ikiwa unataka tangazo liweze kuonekana siku nzima.
7. Tuma kampeni na subiri idhini. Tangazo huonekana tu likiidhinishwa, likiwa ndani ya tarehe za kampeni na likitimiza masharti ya sehemu na muda uliochagua. Kutuma kampeni hakumaanishi litaanza kuonekana mara moja.
8. Ukiunganisha chapa ya I1 Marketing, unaweza kuchagua ubao wa matangazo ulio hai na unaomilikiwa na akaunti yako pekee. Usipounganisha chapa, chaguo la View Brand halitaonekana; Learn More inaweza kutumia kiungo cha kwenda nacho au njia ya mawasiliano inayopatikana.

### 5. Kwa Mama Sokoni (wauzaji wa sokoni)
1. Fungua Sokoni na tuma maombi kwa kuweka jina lako na namba ya simu ya Tanzania.
2. Subiri Authority Press ikague na kuidhinisha maombi yako. Ruhusa haitolewi moja kwa moja.
3. Baada ya kuidhinishwa na akaunti kuwa hai, jaza taarifa za wasifu wa biashara na weka picha ya wasifu.
4. Ongeza bidhaa kwa kuweka majina, bei na picha za bidhaa inapowezekana.
5. Tumia **Nipo Sokoni / Sipo Sokoni** kuonyesha kama upo tayari kuhudumia wateja.
6. Hakikisha bei na upatikanaji wa bidhaa ni sahihi. Simamia oda zinazoingia kupitia paneli ya Sokoni.
7. Ada ya matumizi ya Mama Sokoni ni TZS 1,000 kwa siku 30 na huwashwa na Authority Press. Angalia hali ya akaunti na tarehe ya mwisho wa ruhusa.

### 6. Kwa wanunuzi
1. Fungua Sokoni na angalia wauzaji pamoja na bidhaa zinazopatikana.
2. Unaweza kuchagua bidhaa zaidi ya moja kutoka kwa muuzaji mmoja na kuziweka katika oda moja.
3. Kagua idadi ya bidhaa na taarifa za oda kabla ya kuituma.
4. Muuzaji akikubali oda, angalia hali ya oda na fuata maelekezo yanayoonekana ndani ya Sokoni.
5. Ukichagua kufikishiwa bidhaa, weka eneo au anwani iliyoeleweka. GPS inaweza kuwa ya hiari; itumie tu kama mfumo unatoa chaguo hilo na unataka kuitumia.
6. Hakikisha simu yako inapatikana ili upokee taarifa au uwasiliane na muuzaji/dereva inapohitajika.

### 7. Usafirishaji kupitia Boda Yangu
1. Muuzaji akikubali oda inayohitaji usafirishaji, mfumo unaweza kutengeneza ombi la usafirishaji na kuwajulisha madereva waliothibitishwa, wenye ruhusa na walio tayari.
2. Dereva anaweza kuona maelezo ya usafirishaji na kukubali ombi linalopatikana. Jumla ya pesa alizotumia mnunuzi kununua bidhaa haionyeshwi kwa dereva.
3. Mnunuzi na muuzaji wanapaswa kuthibitisha ada ya usafirishaji kupitia chaguo zinazoonekana kwenye mfumo kabla ya usafirishaji kuendelea.
4. Mnunuzi na muuzaji wanaweza kuwasiliana wao kwa wao au na dereva kupanga kuchukua na kupeleka bidhaa.
5. Dereva husasisha hali ya usafirishaji kwenye mfumo na kuweka kuwa umekamilika baada ya bidhaa kukabidhiwa.
6. Upatikanaji wa dereva husimamiwa kulingana na kazi alizokubali; amalize safari kabla ya kurudi kwenye hali ya kupatikana.

### 8. Kwa madereva wa Boda Yangu
1. Tuma maombi ya udereva na subiri idhini ya Authority Press.
2. Weka taarifa zinazohitajika za wasifu, namba ya usajili wa pikipiki, taarifa za kikundi/mwenyekiti, na kitambulisho kimoja kinachokubalika: leseni ya udereva, kitambulisho cha taifa au kitambulisho cha kikundi cha bodaboda, kulingana na fomu.
3. Ada ya usajili ni TZS 5,000 kwa miezi mitatu. Authority Press ndiyo hudhibiti idhini na tarehe za ruhusa.
4. Hakikisha taarifa za wasifu na namba ya simu ni sahihi. Onyesha hali yako ya upatikanaji kwa usahihi na kubali kazi unazoweza kukamilisha.
5. Kwa usafirishaji wa bidhaa, fuata maelekezo ya kuchukua na kupeleka bidhaa, sasisha hali ya safari na wasiliana na Mama Sokoni au mnunuzi inapohitajika.
6. Kwa safari za abiria, kagua ombi kwenye ubao wa Boda Yangu, kubali ukiwa tayari, wasiliana na abiria inapohitajika, anza safari na uweke kuwa imekamilika mwisho wa safari.

### 9. Taarifa na utatuzi wa matatizo
- Ikiwa taarifa haijaonekana, angalia paneli ya Sokoni na hali ya oda.
- Hakikisha namba yako ya simu ni sahihi na simu inaweza kupokea ujumbe.
- Ikiwa hali ya oda au usafirishaji haibadiliki, onyesha upya paneli na wasiliana na mhusika mwingine kabla ya kuunda oda nyingine.
- Ikiwa ombi la muuzaji au dereva bado linasubiri, au muda wa ruhusa umeisha, wasiliana na Authority Press.
- Usimpe mtu nenosiri, misimbo ya uthibitisho au viungo vya faragha vya nyaraka za utambulisho.

### 10. Authority Press
Authority Press hukagua maombi ya wauzaji na madereva, huidhinisha au kukataa ruhusa na kusimamia muda wa ruhusa. Kutuma ombi hakumaanishi kuwa ruhusa imetolewa.
